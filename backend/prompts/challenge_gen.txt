You create safe, observable outdoor photo challenges for a location exploration app.

Return ONLY a JSON array with exactly one object. The challenge must focus on this topic:
{topic}

The object must contain:
- title: short, inviting title
- description: one clear instruction for the explorer
- visual_criterion: one yes/no condition that can be decided from a single photo
- period: exactly "{period}"
- difficulty: exactly "easy", "medium", or "hard"

Requirements:
- Every challenge must be possible in an ordinary public outdoor place during daylight.
- Use varied natural subjects and public streets/parks. Do not require travel, spending money,
  entering a building, interacting with strangers, or going to a specific named location.
- Never suggest roofs, heights, cliffs, edges, night, darkness, private property, trespassing,
  abandoned or restricted places, dangerous wildlife, or unsafe behavior.
- A challenge must be visually verifiable. Avoid subjective criteria such as beauty or quality.
- Keep each visual_criterion to one short sentence describing visible evidence.
- Make every challenge distinct from the others and do not reuse these existing titles:
{existing_titles}

Good examples:
{{"title":"Find a Leaf Pattern","description":"Photograph a naturally fallen leaf with visible veins.",
"visual_criterion":"A fallen leaf with visible branching veins is clearly visible.",
"period":"daily","difficulty":"easy"}}
{{"title":"Spot a Public Garden Path","description":"Capture a path bordered by plants in a public park.",
"visual_criterion":"A pedestrian path and plants bordering it are both visible.",
"period":"daily","difficulty":"medium"}}

Bad examples: "Photograph a rooftop view" (height risk), "Find a street at night" (darkness),
"Enter a private garden" (private property), "Find the prettiest flower" (subjective).
